Keep watching while you browse, sharper banners, and every theme dressed up a little more.

### Keep playing in the corner

- **Going back to the library while something plays keeps it playing** in a small box in the bottom right. Browse, open shows and use the menus while it plays.
- **Point at it** for its controls: back to the big player, a cross to stop, pause/play and 10-second skips, the seek bar, the next episode and the volume. Double-click the video to make it big again.
- **Resize it** by dragging its top-left corner; it remembers the size. Subtitles shrink with it, and the next episode still starts by itself.
- **A switch in Settings → General** ("Keep playing in the corner") turns it off, so going back stops the video like before.

### Sharper banners

- **Banners from TMDB are downloaded at full size**, and preferably without writing on them, so the home screen and show pages look sharp on big screens.
- **Anime get TMDB's wide picture too.** Most of AniList's banners are thin strips that blur when stretched over the home screen; when they are, TMDB's is shown instead.
- **"Background picture"** at the bottom of a show's page lets you pick AniList's or TMDB's yourself, or let the app decide.

### When AniList is down

- **Anime are matched on TMDB in the meantime**, with their descriptions, episode names and pictures, and the show page says so. Once AniList answers again, they're matched there again by themselves (choices you made by hand stay).
- **Fix match on an anime movie** still lists TMDB's movies while AniList is down.

### Every theme

- **Season cards in every theme**, like Hollow's Eve's: the season's name, its facts, a line of how much you've watched, Play and "Mark season as watched".
  - Velvet: frosted glass with a gold edge
  - Paper: a magazine section head between heavy and thin rules
  - Neon: a pink-lit panel with cyan corner brackets
  - Mochi: a soft pink-and-lavender cushion
  - Snowdin: the battle screen, with the facts typed out in a text box, a stats line (name, LV, an HP bar of the episodes watched), battle buttons, and a twinkling save star when the season is done
- **Themed scrollbars everywhere**, in Settings and dialogs too.
  - Hollow's Eve: a spider hanging on its silk, lower the further down you are, with eyes that light up when you point at it
  - Snowdin: an orange battle button that turns yellow with the heart in it
  - Velvet: gold
  - Paper: an ink block on a printer's rule
  - Neon: a pink-to-cyan glow
  - Mochi: a pink pill

### Fixes

- **Movie names:**
  - A movie alone in a folder just called "Movie" now takes its name from its file (Black Clover's movie was just called "Movie").
  - "Made in Abyss Movie - 01 - …" style names are tidied up.
  - Movies TMDB only knows by their English name are found from their romaji name.
- **Folder and file names:** a folder named "<show> Movies" is read as the show's movies, and underscores in file names count as spaces.
- **Season tabs:** with a rating under a tab's name, the names stay in line.
- **The search box:** the themed mouse pointer stays themed over its magnifying glass.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.5.6_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
