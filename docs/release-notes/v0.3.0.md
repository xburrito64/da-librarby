Previews on the seek bar, My List, a watch-time page, shuffle, a sleep timer, smoother screens, and a lot more for Snowdin.

### Watching

- **Previews on the seek bar:** point at the seek bar and a little picture shows that moment. They're made in the background while you watch, and kept for the videos you played most recently, so watching again has them straight away.
- **Shuffle** a show from its page or the right-click menu: random episodes, one after another.
- **Sleep timer** (the moon in the player): at the end of this episode, or in 15 to 90 minutes. The sound fades out gently and the video pauses.
- **Frame by frame:** **,** and **.** step one frame back or forward.

### Finding things

- **My List:** save shows and movies for later from their page or the right-click menu; they get their own row on the home screen.
- **Filters** on the Anime, Shows and Movies pages: not started, in progress, finished, new episodes, My List, and genre.
- **Extras, sorted:** extras are listed under headings taken from their folders (Season 1, Season 2… or the folder's own name), and a movie's extras show with the movies.
- **"Where you are"** on show pages ("Season 3 · 4 of 12 episodes left · about 1 h 30 min"), and a tick on seasons you've finished.
- **Arrow keys** move between covers, buttons and episodes, and Enter opens or plays.

### New pages and moments

- **Watch time:** how much you watched this month, this year or ever, your day streak, your most watched shows, and when you usually watch.
- **Finishing a season** gets a little celebration back in the library.
- **Smoother screens:** a cover grows into its show page when you open it, and shrinks back when you leave.

### Snowdin

- **Heart pointer:** the mouse pointer becomes the heart. Pick its colour from the seven soul colours or any colour you like, for every heart or just the pointer.
- **Battle start:** pressing Play starts like an encounter: the screen goes dark, the heart flashes where you clicked and bursts open onto the video.
- **Background music:** put your own music files in Snowdin's own-files folder (Settings → Appearance → Your own files). One loops; several take turns. It pauses during videos and while the app is in the background.
- The watch-time page is the **STAT** screen, with LV, EXP and NEXT.
- Your own-files folder also takes sounds for the battle start.

### Fixes

- The whole "Start over" button in the player can be clicked.
- The snow comes back after watching something.
- "In progress" only counts shows you've really started.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.3.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
