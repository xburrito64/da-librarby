Movie pages, movies from shows on their own pages, an update notice, easier first setup, and a hanging town sign in Snowdin.

### Movies

- **Movie pages** got a lot bigger: the artwork fills the window (Paper prints it as a big photo, Mochi as a tall picture card), with the tagline, the director, and a line with the length, when it would end if you started now, the picture quality and the audio and subtitle languages.
- **About:** everything about the film (release date and country, original title and language, age rating, who wrote it, did the music and produced it, studios, budget, box office, rating), about your copy (file size, picture, every audio and subtitle track, chapters, when it was added), and about your watching (how far you are, time watched, first and last watched). Movies from a collection show which of its films you have.
- **Cast** with photos and the characters they play.
- **More like this:** other movies (and shows) from your library that are alike: the same collection, studio, show or genres.
- **Movies kept in a show's folder** (Naruto's movies, Jujutsu Kaisen 0, the SpongeBob movies…) are now movies of their own: they're on the Movies page ("2021 · Jujutsu Kaisen"), have a full movie page with a link back to their show, and the show's Movies & Specials tab opens them. Their watch history stays.

### Easier to set up and keep up to date

- **Getting a TMDB key** is explained step by step in Settings → Online info, with buttons to the right TMDB pages. Without a key, a note in the library says why shows and movies have no covers yet.
- **Update notice:** the app asks GitHub now and then whether there's a newer version and says so in a small note with a download button. Turn it off, or check by hand, in Settings → About.

### Snowdin

- **The town sign** hangs from ropes and swings when you click it, knocking snow off (it builds up again over a few minutes). Sometimes the dog is asleep on it…
- **Check** on movie pages: the game's CHECK, with the movie's HP, ATK and DEF.

### Fixes

- Shows whose seasons are in story-arc folders ("01. Hunter Exam Arc") are one show with the arcs as tabs, and their episodes get their info.
- The folder's year picks the right version of a show ("Hunter x Hunter (2011)", not the 1999 series).
- Episode titles written with "×" match their info.
- A movie among a show's specials (SAO's Ordinal Scale) is recognised as a movie.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.4.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
