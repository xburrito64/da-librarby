Hollow's Eve: a Halloween theme, just in time for October.

### Hollow's Eve

A pumpkin patch on Halloween night, behind your whole library. Pick it in Settings → Appearance, or let it take over by itself every October.

- **The home screen:** a moonlit hill with a crooked haunted house (its door creaks open, its windows light up), trees that each grow their own way (an owl's tree, a grumpy tree with a face, a tire swing), bats flitting past the moon, an old cemetery with an iron gate and a black cat on its pillar, and a patch full of jack-o'-lanterns, each with its own face, size and colour.
- **Under the patch:** a slice of earth with roots, worms, old bones, glowing mushrooms, a coffin that creaks open, a sleeping skeleton that wakes up and waves, and a secret candy stash to find.
- **Show and movie pages:** a graveyard below the title where every tombstone is different (and a ghost peeks out from behind the big one), and a Gothic family tomb with candles and an iron gate.
- **Everything answers a click:** pumpkins, the house's door, the ghost, the bats, the spider, the cat (it also follows your pointer with its eyes when you come close), and the falling leaves, which you can flick away.
- **Falling leaves** drift and tumble like real ones, and now and then something happens among them: a gust of wind, a little whirl, a leaf landing on something.
- **Little friends on the covers:** some shows have a pumpkin, a ghost, a crow, a black cat, a spider, a witch's hat and more sitting on their cover, each doing something when you point at it. A candle shows how far you are into a show (it burns down as you watch), a tombstone marks finished ones, and a ghost brings news of new episodes.
- **Stone tablets** hold the home banner's description, a show's description and its About. Each one is carved on its own, with its own chips and cracks, moss, ivy, mushrooms, cobwebs and lichen, and now and then something rare: a carving, a burnt-down candle, a fallen leaf or a snail. A show always gets the same stone.
- **The episode list** reads like a storybook: each season opens on a tablet with how many episodes you've watched and about how long is left, plus Play or Resume right where you are. Episodes get big chapter numbers and their length, watched ones get a wax seal, and the next one sits on its own tablet with a lit candle.
- **Fonts to choose:** the theme's title font (seven to pick from) and the tablets' font (six), each shown in its own lettering in Settings. IM Fell English is the default for both.
- **Real sounds:** plucked strings, a glockenspiel, an organ, creaks, bats and bones, all free recordings (CC0). Theme sounds now only play while the app is in front, and fade out when you switch away.

The other themes look just as they did.

### Fixes

- **Naruto Shippuden** (and other follow-up series) is recognised as its own show instead of being mixed up with the series before it.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.5.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
