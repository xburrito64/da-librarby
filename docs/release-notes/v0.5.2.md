Covers in carved stone, spooky middle-click scrolling, and pictures for episodes that had none.

### Hollow's Eve

- **Covers in stone:** every cover, and every episode in Continue watching, sits in a chipped stone frame, carved like the description tablets. Each show has its own frame, and now and then one has a cobweb, a spider, ivy, moss, grass, mushrooms or a crack on it. Point at a cover and it rises, its frame catching the candlelight.
  - The names under the covers are in carved lettering, and **New** and **Up next** are little stone tags.
- **Scrolling with the middle mouse button** uses your pointer's style: the ghost floats between little arrows, then dives down or rises up as you scroll. The reaching ghost, the will-o'-the-wisp, the jack-o'-lantern and the bat each have their own. Scrolling a row of covers sideways glides smoothly.
- **Where you type**, the text cursor matches your pointer too: a sheet hem for the ghost, a flame for the wisp, a stem for the jack-o'-lantern, a tiny bat for the bat.

### Snowdin

- **Scrolling with the middle mouse button** with the heart pointer on: the heart sits between pixel arrows, and while you scroll it turns into the blue soul, as when gravity pulls it in the game.
- **Where you type**, the text cursor is a pixel bar in your heart colour.

### Every theme

- **Pictures for every episode:** an episode that has no picture from anywhere now gets one taken from the video itself, about a third of the way in. It's made once, when the episode comes into view, and kept.

### Better matching

- **Episode numbers before a bracketed tag with the quality inside**, like `KILL la KILL - 03 [BD 1080p x265 OPUS][Anipakku]`, are read again. Before, these episodes got no info and the name ended in "BD".
- **Anime specials** are found on TMDB by what they're matched to on AniList, so specials like Black Clover's Jump Festa and Thanksgiving Festa episodes get their names, descriptions and pictures.
- **A special numbered on from the show's last episode**, like Kill la Kill's episode 25 in a Specials folder, gets the OVA that followed.
- **A tab you matched by hand** (like Black Clover's Petit Clover) gives its files the one-episode specials picked for it, with or without an episode number, when no numbered episode uses them.

Anime are looked up again once after updating, to apply these.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.5.2_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
