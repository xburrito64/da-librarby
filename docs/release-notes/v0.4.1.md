Everything grows with the window, smarter sorting of specials and openings, and a livelier top bar in Snowdin.

### Everywhere

- **Interface size:** in a big window (or full screen on a large monitor) everything gets bigger by itself: text, covers, buttons and pictures. Settings → Appearance has an **Interface size** slider to make it all bigger or smaller, and a switch to stop it growing with the window.

### Sorting your folders

- **OVAs and OADs** are specials: folders named "OVA", "OAD" or "ONA", and files numbered like "OVA 3 - Memory Days", "OAD 2" or "SP 1", go into the Specials tab with their own number and title (no longer as movies).
- **Openings and endings** ("OP And ED" folders, files like "OP 001 …", "ED 3", "NCOP") are extras, not movies.
- Something in a show's folder that's far too small to be a movie is listed as an extra.
- **"Movie 01"** in a movie's file name no longer gets in the way of finding its info ("Fairy Tail - Movie 01 - The Phoenix Priestess" is found as "Fairy Tail - The Phoenix Priestess").

### Snowdin

- **The eaves:** a snowy roof beam runs along the top, with icicles. The town sign hangs from it, and the string of lights is tied to the sign's rope and hangs from nails in soft loops that sway, and get tugged along when you swing the sign.
- **Bulbs to play with:** point at one to make it glow, click it to play a note (higher towards the right). Run the pointer along a row of bulbs for a little light show. Sometimes one is burnt out, and once in a while the dog sits on the sign chewing the cord…
- **Snow:** the sign's snow is knocked off in uneven lumps (five swings clear it) and builds back up softly; every cover has a snow pile of its own.
- Clicking the sign no longer takes you back Home: it's just for swinging.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.4.1_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
