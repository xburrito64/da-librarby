Spooky mouse pointers, a haunted video player, and a watch time page with facts and charms to collect.

### Watch time (every theme)

- **Facts:** under the numbers, a row of little facts about the month, year or all time you're looking at: the day with the most watching (and what you watched), how many episodes you finished after midnight, your favourite day and hour, and your biggest binge of one show in one sitting.
- **Charms to collect:** eight badges to earn from everything you've ever watched:

  | Charm | What it takes |
  | --- | --- |
  | Night Owl | 10 episodes after midnight |
  | Marathon | 5 episodes of a show in one sitting |
  | Seven Nights | watching 7 days in a row |
  | Movie Night | a movie finished after 22:00 |
  | Full Moon | 30 hours in one month |
  | Century | 100 episodes |
  | Film Buff | 10 movies |
  | Halloween | watching something on October 31 |

  The ones still to earn show how far along you are. Each theme shows them in its own style.

### Hollow's Eve

- **Mouse pointers:** pick yours in Settings → Appearance:
  - **Pointer:** the graveyard's little ghost, a ghost reaching out, a will-o'-the-wisp, a tiny jack-o'-lantern, a bat, or Windows' own.
  - **Pointer over buttons:** the ghost pointing, a bony hand, a witch's hand, a ghostly hand, or Windows' own.
  - **Pointer turns into what it points at** (can be switched off): a key at the tomb's gate, a bone over the skeleton, a paw over the cat, a door over the house, BOO over the ghost, a bat over the bats, and more. Secrets stay secret: they just get a sparkle.
- **The tomb's candles:** the little ghost blows them out. A blown-out candle can now be lit again with a match, or it lights itself after a while like before.
- **The video player:**
  - The seek bar is a burning fuse, with ash behind it, sparks where it's burning and little gravestones for the chapters.
  - Purple fog and a graveyard along the bottom.
  - Play glows like a lit pumpkin, and the clock has a pocket watch.
  - The preview and the menus sit on stone tablets.
  - When you pause, a jack-o'-lantern glows in the middle.
  - The small player in the corner stays as plain as before.
- **The watch time page:**
  - The numbers and panels are carved stone tablets, with drawings: an hourglass, tally marks, a film reel, a candle for each day of your streak.
  - Day by day is a row of candles, as tall as you watched.
  - When you watch is a moon clock with your witching hour.
  - Most watched has burning fuses and a witch hat on number one.
  - The charms are wax seals on a string.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.5.1_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
