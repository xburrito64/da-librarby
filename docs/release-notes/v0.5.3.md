An info card for every episode, every time you watched it, a player that gets out of the way, and extras that are really specials moving where they belong.

### Episode cards

- **An "i" on every episode:** point at an episode and a little "i" appears in its corner. In Hollow's Eve it's carved into a chipped stone and glows like a candle when you point at it, in Snowdin it turns yellow like a chosen option, and the other themes light it in their own colour. Right-click → **More info** opens the same card, for extras too.
- **The card** shows the episode's picture, name, full description and air date, with buttons to resume, start over or open the file's folder. In Hollow's Eve it's one big carved stone tablet. Click beside it, the X or press Esc to close it.
- **Every time you watched it:** each viewing of 5 minutes or more gets its own line, with when and how far ("to the end", "stopped at 10:00"), plus how often you've watched it. Quick looks to check the quality don't count. Your earlier watching is pieced together from your watch time, by day.
- **What's in the file:** resolution, codec and profile (e.g. HEVC Main 10), bit depth, frame rate, bitrate, SDR / HDR10 / HLG / Dolby Vision, colour space (BT.709, BT.2020...), primaries, transfer, colour range and chroma, and every audio and subtitle track with its language, channels, codec, bitrate and whether it's the default or forced. Whatever a file doesn't say just isn't shown.
- **Movie pages** show the same details about the file in their About section.

### The player

- **Move the mouse to another screen** and everything over the picture fades away, paused or not: the controls, the title, the theme's pause look and any messages. Bring it back and they return.

### Better matching

- **Extras that are really specials** move into the show's Specials tab when TMDB lists them there, with their names, descriptions and pictures: Gravity Falls' shorts, South Park's specials, Frieren's mini episodes, Attack on Titan's Chibi Theater and more. They're found by their name, or by the name of their folder in front ("Gag Reel" in "Shop at Home with Mr Mystery"). A video holding several specials, named part by part with " + " between them, gets all of them. Extras added or renamed later are checked again by themselves.
- **Complete-series releases** like Gurren Lagann's: numbered folders ("01. Series", "02. Movies") are read by what they hold, OVA and special folders among the extras become specials, a numbered side series gets a tab of its own, a range like "Episode 9-15" is read as one file holding those episodes, and specials are found by the number they go by ("Episode 5.5") or by the end of their name.
- **A tab you matched by hand** gives its specials by name first.

Shows are looked up again once after updating, to apply these.

### Fixed

- In Hollow's Eve, the hidden things on the home screen (the coffin, the skeleton, the tomb, the cat...) no longer answer clicks or change the pointer from behind a show's page or a dialog. Snowdin's lights and trees don't either.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.5.3_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
