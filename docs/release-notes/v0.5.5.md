Rate everything you watch, your way.

### Ratings

- **Rate shows, movies, seasons and episodes.** Next to a show's or movie's name, in a season's facts, on an episode's card (the "i"), or with a right-click → **Rate…** on any cover or episode.
- **Six styles**, in Settings → Ratings:
  - **1–5 stars**, halves too
  - **1–10 score** with decimals, like 8.7: drag it, scroll on it, tap − / + for 0.1 at a time, or just type it
  - **Thumbs**: not for me, liked it, loved it
  - **Tier list**: S down to F
  - **Theme stickers**: five of the theme's own (lit jack-o'-lanterns, Undertale save stars, gold foil, red ink stamps, neon stars, puffy pink stars)
  - **Feelings**: one sticker of the theme's own (a sad, a smiling or a blazing jack-o'-lantern; your SOUL broken, whole or full of DETERMINATION; popcorn knocked over, full or overflowing; and more)
  
  Every style shows the same rating, so switching never changes or loses one.
- **On the covers**: a row of stickers, only when pointed at, a sticker on the corner, or off. In Hollow's Eve the corner sticker is a small carved stone with the rating glowing in it.
- **On the season tabs**: a sticker on the tab's corner, the rating under its name, or off. And a small rating on episode pictures (a switch).
- **"How was it?"**: finish a show, a season or a movie, and a small note on the home screen asks you to rate it. "Not now" makes it go away.
- **Sort by your rating**, a **"Your favourites"** row on Home (a switch in Settings, off at first), and your favourites and average on the Watch time page.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.5.5_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
