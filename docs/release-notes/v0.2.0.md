A new theme, a mini player, and a few smoothings.

### Highlights

- **Snowdin**, a fifth theme: a cozy snowy pixel town at night, after the game this app's name comes from.
  - Snowdin itself sits under the spotlight: log cabins with warm windows, the decorated tree, lamp posts, a snowman, string lights and a banner welcoming you to the Librarby. Show pages get the snowy forest and a sentry station.
  - Snow falls gently behind everything, pixel text throughout, and a little red heart follows whatever you point at.
  - Descriptions type themselves out in the game's text boxes, Continue watching is a row of save files, and the player's seek bar is an HP bar.
  - Little interface sounds (off during videos), with their own on/off switch and volume.
  - A few secrets to find.
  - **Your own files:** Settings → Appearance can point you to a folder where you can drop in your own font and sound files to use instead of the built-in ones. They stay on your PC.
- **Mini player:** the button next to Fullscreen (or **P**) shrinks the video into a small window that stays on top of other windows. Drag it around, resize it from its edges, and double-click or press **Esc** to go back to the big player.
- **Themes can do more:** besides colours and fonts, a theme can now bring its own options, sounds, wording, pixel icons and decorations. The theme guide in `src/theme/README.md` explains how.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.2.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
