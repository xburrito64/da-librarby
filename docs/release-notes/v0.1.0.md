The first release of Da Librarby: a Netflix-style library and player for the anime, shows and movies on your own drives.

### Highlights

- **Point it at your folders or drives** and it sorts anime, shows and movies by itself, including seasons, specials, movies inside show folders, extras and unusual numbering.
- **Covers, descriptions and episode pictures** from AniList (anime) and TMDB (shows and movies; needs a free key), saved locally. Wrong matches can be fixed by hand.
- **Built-in mpv player**: chapters, audio/subtitle tracks remembered per show, subtitle size and position, speed, skip intro/credits, screenshots, and automatic next episode.
- **Watch history**: resume where you stopped, watched marks, "Continue watching", and "New" badges.
- **Search, sorting, right-click menus**, "Surprise me" and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Four themes**: Velvet, Paper, Neon and Mochi.
- **One Pace support**: official episode titles and descriptions from the One Pace guide.
- Finds new files when you switch to the app, notices drives being plugged in or removed, and can start with Windows.

### Installing

Download `Da Librarby_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**. Then add your folders in **Settings → Library folders** and, for shows and movies, a TMDB key in **Settings → Online info** (the README explains how to get one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
