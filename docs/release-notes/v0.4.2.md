Cleaner episode names, and Snowdin's woods come to life.

### Everywhere

- **Episode names:** files named like "Attack.on.Titan.S03E01.GERMAN.Dubbed.DL.1080p…" no longer show "GERMAN Dubbed DL" as the episode's name. Language and dub tags after the episode number are skipped, and the episode's real name is shown instead. Nothing needs renaming: it's fixed on the next scan of your folders.

### Snowdin

- **The woods:** every row of trees stands on its own snowy hill, so they're planted in the snow instead of floating behind it, and they rise and fall with the hills. On the left, where the text is, the forest opens into a clearing, and a trail of footprints leads into the trees.
- **Trees to play with:** point at a tree and it shakes the snow off its branches; it builds back up over a minute or so. Now and then a clump drops off a branch by itself… and once in a while something watches from the dark between the trees.
- **The sign's ropes** look like real twisted rope, and they're tied around the roof beam, so swinging the sign no longer shows where they end.
- **Sharper hearts:** the hearts (the colour choices in Settings, and beside whatever you point at) stay crisp at any interface size.

### Installing

Download `Da.Librarby_0.4.2_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. It updates an existing installation and keeps your library, watch history and settings. Windows may say the publisher is unknown because the installer isn't signed: click **More info → Run anyway**.

New here? Add your folders in the app, then follow Settings → Online info to get a free TMDB key for show and movie info (anime works without one).

Windows 10/11, 64-bit.
