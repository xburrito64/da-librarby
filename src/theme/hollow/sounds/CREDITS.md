# Hollow's Eve's sounds

Real recordings, trimmed and levelled for the theme. All are CC0 (public domain): free to use,
change and share, no credit required. Where they came from, gratefully:

## Pointing at something (`move`)

- `move.ogg`: a small wooden tick. VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0).

## Picking something (`select`)

- `select.ogg`: two plucked violin notes, going up. VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0).

## Going back (`back`)

- `back.ogg`: a low plucked cello note. VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0).

## Saved / marked as watched (`save`)

- `save.ogg`: a spooky little glockenspiel tune (minor, four notes). VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0).

## Treat! / found a secret (`treat`)

- `treat.ogg`: a plucked-strings jingle, bouncy. Kenney (CC0).

## Nothing found / can't do that (`nope`)

- `nope.ogg`: a tuba going “bwomp bwomp” (down). VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0).

## Trick! (`trick`)

- `trick.ogg`: organ “dun dun dunnn”, then the chord. VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0).

## Whoever's in the coffin (`moan`)

- `moan.ogg`: a ghostly moan. Freesound #643741, “Ghostly Moan.mp3” by SnowFightStudios (CC0).

## The ghost, startled (`boo`)

- `boo-1.ogg`: a tiny startled “hoh!” (voice made small and high). Freesound #242606, “gasp HOH!.wav” by Reitanna (CC0).
- `boo-2.ogg`: a small surprised “oh!” (voice made small and high). Freesound #242677, “surprised 'oh!'.wav” by Reitanna (CC0).
- `boo-3.ogg`: a slide whistle zipping up. Freesound #497092, “FX swanee whistle up.wav” by v0idation (CC0).
- `boo-4.ogg`: a cute squeak. Freesound #857748, “Cute Computer Squeak” by qubodup (CC0).

## The spider scurrying up (`skitter`)

- `skitter.ogg`: something crawling, fast. Freesound #266014, “Fast Crawling Bu” by dasrealized (CC0).

## The skeleton waking up (`rattle`)

- `rattle.ogg`: bones knocking together. Freesound #188034, “Bones 2” by AntumDeluge (CC0).

## A pumpkin bonked (`bonk`)

- `bonk.ogg`: a wooden knock. Kenney (CC0).

## The house's door creaking open (`creak`)

- `creak-2.ogg`: a door creaking (2). Freesound #219492, “Door - Creak 02.wav” by JarredGibb (CC0).
- `creak-3.ogg`: a door creaking (3). Freesound #377552, “Wooden Creak” by Mafon2 (CC0).
- `creak-4.ogg`: a door creaking (4). Freesound #125957, “Opening a creaking door” by Ryding (CC0).

- `flutter-2.ogg`: wings flapping (2). Freesound #240476, “wings.wav” by Godowan (CC0).
- `flutter-3.ogg`: wings flapping (3). Freesound #240469, “wings2.wav” by Godowan (CC0).

- `scrape-2.ogg`: a wooden chest sliding. Freesound #146805, “Wood Chest Slid.3.wav” by kforetek (CC0).
